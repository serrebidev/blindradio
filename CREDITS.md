# Credits

BlindRadio exists because internet radio apps are mouse-first and stations are found by scrolling through endless lists. This one tunes like an analogue dial: the station under focus plays at once, and soft hiss fills the moment between stations.

Third-party projects and sources it uses:

- yt-dlp (with Deno) for the audio of YouTube and Twitch live streams added by link.
- minisign verifies every download before it installs.
- Radio Browser, TuneIn, iHeartRadio, Icecast, SHOUTcast, Internet-Radio, IPRD, and Recommended Radio Streams for the station directories.

Questions and bug reports are welcome in the [SerrebiProjects Telegram group](https://t.me/SerrebiProjects). More serrebi projects: [projects.serrebiradio.com](https://projects.serrebiradio.com).
